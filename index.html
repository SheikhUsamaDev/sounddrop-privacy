Privacy Policy

App Name: SoundDrop
Last Updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SoundDrop ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

SoundDrop does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app operates locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account or log in.

2. Audio Downloads

When you paste a URL into the app, the app sends that URL to a third-party audio processing service to retrieve audio content. We do not store or log the URLs you submit.

3. Advertising

SoundDrop uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings under Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Third-Party Services

SoundDrop uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – advertising
  • RapidAPI – audio processing

5. Data Storage

All downloaded audio files are stored locally on your device only. We have no access to files stored on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

SoundDrop is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
